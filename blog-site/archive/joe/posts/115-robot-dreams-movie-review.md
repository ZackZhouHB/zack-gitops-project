**Robot Dreams review:**

A dog lived alone in New York city, dreaming about friends. Everything changed when he bought a robot **companion** from an advertisement. The two quickly became **inseparable** friends.

One day, the robot was broken at the ocean beach, and the dog tried every way but still had to leave his friend there until the beach reopens next year.

The robot had 3 dreams to be reunited with the dog, unfortunately he was taken and smashed in the scrapyard. In the meantime, the dog **struggled** to make new friends and eventually bought another yellow robot.

Luckily, the robot was picked and repaired by a raccoon, and they developed a new **relationship** soon.

One afternoon, the robot spotted the dog again, he decided to keep all their good memories and move on to each other's new life.

I love this story's dialogue-free, **emotional** scenes and music, which made me cry. It reminded me of another movie "Big Hero 6", a robot companion Baymax. I really like the song "September" from this movie.

"Robot Dreams" talks about friendship, love and **loss**, it will inspire anyone who had the same experience from the story and encourage people to grow up and move on when they get **separated**.

I think Robot Dreams is one of my favorite movies, I will rate it 7.8/10.
